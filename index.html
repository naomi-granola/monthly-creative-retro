Glossary — what each vp- tag means
Notepad — The capture itself — records the conversation into notes you can trust, jotting optional. Merges the old vp-notetaking tag.
Be present — The emotional payoff of being fully in the room — not splitting focus between listening and writing.
No bots — No bot joins the call; the meeting stays between the people in it (human, not stealth).
Around the meeting — The whole before/during/after arc in one tool — "around the meeting, not in it."
Prep — Walking in prepared before — briefs, "walk in ready," pulling context before you join.
Follow-ups — After the meeting — follow-up drafts, client-ready summaries, speed-to-recap.
Recall — Retrieving/querying what was said — chat across past meetings, "what did I miss?"
Integrations — Works with your other tools — Slack, Notion, CRM, MCP/API ("feeds your stack").
Cross-platform — Works anywhere — any meeting tool, phone, in-person, mobile, offline.
Privacy — Data security / "just for me" — sensitive 1:1s, saved on your device.
Social proof — Others vouch — testimonials, referrals, a real shared summary. Was vp-trust.
The heaviest-funded messages are the least efficient. Case to shift test budget toward the under-funded angles. (CP-account proxy, not CP-FVM; a North-Star vp read needs Motion FVM by creative.)

Does the Instagram Reels skew hurt meeting quality?
No — the one creative leading on Reels converts just as well. C94_g1_v1 · yassixiong (Gravitas, Partnership) is the only Top-10 creative whose lead placement is Instagram Reels (72%) rather than Feed — but its CP-FVM is £90 and its CPC is £0.46, both the cheapest of any Top-10 creative. If a Reels-heavy delivery mix were diluting conversion quality, this ad would show it first — instead it out-converts most of the Feed-led statics and partnership videos above it (£81–£155 CP-FVM). Read: for this account, Reels-skewed delivery isn't a quality-of-signup problem — Meta is finding an efficient audience there, not just a cheap one. Worth deliberately testing more creative into Reels rather than treating the placement mix as something to correct back toward Feed.

Signals

The account is a duplication machine. 2,266 active ad instances resolve to 509 unique creatives (~4.5× across markets/ad sets). Only 467 instances (21%) — or 134 creatives — carry 90% of spend, so a small winners set still runs the account.
New taxonomy is landing — statics lead volume. 264 unique creatives launched this period; Siha (138) and Newform (84) dominate, with Gravitas (2) live as its own source (ex-JM Digital). Statics are 140 of launches vs 99 video / 14 motion.
Daily Doers is the tightest funnel. £1.89 clicks → £74 CP-FVM. Daily Doers pays the most per click (£1.89) — the click price isn't predicting meeting cost, consistent with the framework's spend↔CPA disconnect.
Partnership is the primary spend vehicle. Whitelisted/creator partnership video is £631,332 (46%) of format spend — ahead of brand static — and Creator is now the biggest production source (35%). The account leans heavily on creator-made whitelisted video.
Message efficiency inverts spend. The two most-funded value props — notepad (the capture message, incl. the renamed note-taking token) and be-present — are the least efficient on cost-per-signup (£54–60), while integrations (£41), follow-ups (£42), recall (£45) and no-bots (£46) convert cheaper across every archetype. Busy Mind-ers respond best to integrations/recall, Daily Doers to follow-ups/around-the-meeting, Decision-makers to around-the-meeting/no-bots. Clear case to rebalance test budget toward the under-funded angles — and to get a Motion CP-FVM read on vp to confirm on the North Star.
Legacy still owns the money. Untagged pre-taxonomy winners are 59% of spend. The tagged-archetype batch is efficient but small — the migration is real but not yet dominant in budget.
