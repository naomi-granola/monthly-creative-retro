Glossary — what each vp- tag means
Notepad — The capture itself — records the conversation into notes you can trust, jotting optional. Merges the old vp-notetaking tag.
Be present — The emotional payoff of being fully in the room — not splitting focus between listening and writing.
No bots — No bot joins the call; the meeting stays between the people in it (human, not stealth).
Around the meeting — The whole before/during/after arc in one tool — "around the meeting, not in it."
Prep — Walking in prepared before — briefs, "walk in ready," pulling context before you join.
Follow-ups — After the meeting — follow-up drafts, client-ready summaries, speed-to-recap.
Recall — Retrieving/querying what was said — chat across past meetings, "what did I miss?"
Integrations — Works with your other tools — Slack, Notion, CRM, MCP/API ("feeds your stack").
Cross-platform — Works anywhere — any meeting tool, phone, in-person, mobile, offline.
Privacy — Data security / "just for me" — sensitive 1:1s, saved on your device.
Social proof — Others vouch — testimonials, referrals, a real shared summary. Was vp-trust.
The heaviest-funded messages are the least efficient. Notepad (£308,684, biggest spend — includes the renamed note-taking token) and Be present convert signups at £54–60, while smaller-budget Integrations (£41), No-bots (£46), Recall (£45), Follow-ups (£42) run cheaper — the same pattern holds inside every archetype. Case to shift test budget toward the under-funded angles. (CP-account proxy, not CP-FVM; a North-Star vp read needs Motion FVM by creative.)

Does the Instagram Reels skew hurt meeting quality?
No — the creative leading on Instagram Reels converts just as well. C94_g1_v1 · yassixiong (Gravitas, Partnership) is the Top-10 creative whose lead placement is Instagram Reels (73%) rather than Feed — its CP-FVM is £252 and its CPC is £0.35, against a Feed-led range of £225–£912 CP-FVM among the rest of the Top 10. If a Reels-heavy delivery mix were diluting conversion quality, this ad would show it first — instead it sits inside (or below) the Feed-led range, so Meta is finding an efficient audience there, not just a cheap one. Worth deliberately testing more creative into off-Feed placements rather than treating the mix as something to correct back toward Feed.

Signals

The account is a duplication machine. 2,365 active ad instances resolve to just 501 unique creatives — 90% of spend sits in only 139 of them.
New taxonomy is landing — statics lead volume. 256 creatives launched this period, led by Siha (136) and Newform (78), with statics outpacing video 138 to 93.
Click cost doesn't predict meeting cost. Daily Doers pays the most per click (£1.94) yet lands at the same £67 CP-FVM as cheaper-click archetypes.
Partnership is the primary spend vehicle. Whitelisted/creator video is £647,746 (46% of format spend) — ahead of brand static — with Creator now the biggest production source.
Message efficiency inverts spend. Notepad and Be-present get the most budget but convert signups worst (£54–60), while Integrations, Follow-ups, Recall and No-bots run cheaper across every archetype.
Legacy still owns the money. Untagged pre-taxonomy winners are 59% of spend — the tagged-archetype migration is real but not yet dominant in budget.
